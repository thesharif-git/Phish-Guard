Reading the risk score

Email scans use a 0–100 scale. Open the findings to see which signals contributed.

No finding

The completed checks found little or no risk. This does not guarantee the email is safe.

0–44

Suspicious

Concerning signals were found. Check the findings before trusting the message.

45–74

High risk

Strong phishing signals were found. Avoid acting on the email until you review the evidence.

75–100

A score reflects the checks that completed. Missing evidence is not treated as proof that an email is safe.

Email Authentication Results

When you provide full message headers, the scan can read the authentication outcomes reported there.

SPF

Sending server

Reports whether the header says the sending server was allowed by the sender's domain.

DKIM

Message signature

Reports whether the header says the message signature passed or failed.

DMARC

Sender alignment

Reports whether the header says the visible sender matched the authenticated domain.

These are reported header results, not independent SPF, DKIM, or DMARC verification. A pasted body without headers cannot provide them.

How link inspection works

The scanner combines link patterns with destination details it can retrieve.

Redirects

Shows the destination reached when redirect information is available.

Lookalike names

Checks for spelling and character patterns that resemble familiar domains.

Domain age

Shows registration age when a reliable lookup is available.

Threat sources

External source results appear only when those checks are configured and reachable.

If a check cannot run, the result should show it as unavailable rather than safe.

Scan endpoints

Local endpoint reference for integrations and testing.

POST /api/scan-email Form data · Sign-in required

Send content or an uploaded file. Optional fields include sender, subject, and headers.

POST /api/scan-url JSON

Send a JSON object with a url field, for example {"url": "https://example.com"}.

The email endpoint uses the same signed-in session as the app. For full request and response schemas, open API reference.

Common questions

Answers about scan results, saved history, and account options.

Why was a familiar message flagged?

The sender may be familiar, but its links, wording, or reported authentication results can still raise risk. Open the findings to see what applied.

What is saved in my history?

Scan records include the sender, subject, risk result, and analysis details. Those details may include a preview of the message.

What happens after a high-risk result?

The result shows the risk level and supporting findings. Alerts depend on your notification settings.

Where can I see plan options?

Open Billing & Plan in Settings to see the options available to your account.
