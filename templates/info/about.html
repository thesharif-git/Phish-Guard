Our Mission

Phishing is the #1 cause of data breaches worldwide. It's simple, effective, and devastatingly underestimated. Our mission is to give every individual and every security team the tools to identify and neutralize phishing threats before they cause damage.

PhishGuard combines advanced analysis with real-time global threat intelligence to deliver a verdict in under 3 seconds — no inbox access required, no setup, no complexity.

Speed First

Full analysis delivered in under 3 seconds. Fast enough for the moment a suspicious email arrives.

Privacy by Design

We never ask for inbox access. You paste what you want analyzed — nothing more, nothing less.

Deterministic

Every verdict includes a plain-language breakdown. Know exactly why something is flagged.

What We Analyze

Every scan runs through a multi-stage pipeline: email header authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), sender domain age and reputation, embedded URL cross-checking against VirusTotal, PhishTank, and URLhaus, advanced content analysis for urgency patterns and manipulation language, and a final risk score from 0–100.

Who We're For

PhishGuard serves a broad range of users — from individuals checking a suspicious email before they click, to SOC teams processing thousands of emails a day. Our Free tier requires no registration. Our Team and Business tiers add bulk processing, shared history, role-based access, and SIEM integrations.
