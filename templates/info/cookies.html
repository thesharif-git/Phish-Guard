What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to remember information about your visit, such as your authentication state or preferences.

How PhishGuard Uses Cookies

PhishGuard uses only strictly necessary cookies. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking cookies, or third-party analytics cookies. Our cookies exist solely to keep you securely logged in.

Cookie Name Purpose Duration Type
phishguard_session Maintains your authenticated session. Without this cookie, you would be logged out on every page. Session (expires on browser close) Necessary
csrf_token Protects against Cross-Site Request Forgery attacks by validating that form submissions originate from PhishGuard. Session Necessary

Cookies We Do NOT Use

  • No advertising or retargeting cookies (Google Ads, Facebook Pixel, etc.)
  • No third-party analytics cookies (Google Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.)
  • No social media tracking cookies
  • No persistent fingerprinting technologies

Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling cookies will prevent you from logging into PhishGuard, as session cookies are required for authentication. Anonymous (non-logged-in) scanning will continue to work without cookies.
