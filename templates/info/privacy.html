Overview

PhishGuard ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our email and URL phishing detection service.

By using PhishGuard, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please discontinue use of the service.

Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Submitted Content: Email text, headers, and URLs that you voluntarily paste into our scanner for analysis. This content is processed transiently and stored only to provide you with your scan history.
  • Account Information: If you register, we collect your email address and a hashed password. We do not store plaintext passwords.
  • Usage Data: Browser type, IP address, pages visited, timestamps, and scan metadata (risk score, verdict, scan duration).
  • Cookies and Local Storage: We use session cookies to maintain authentication state. See our Cookie Policy.

We do not connect to, read from, or access your email inbox. All content submitted is provided manually by you.

How We Use Data

  • To analyze submitted content and return a phishing risk verdict.
  • To maintain your scan history and provide audit trail functionality.
  • To improve our AI models and threat intelligence algorithms (using anonymized, aggregated data only).
  • To authenticate your account and manage sessions securely.
  • To send transactional emails (e.g., password reset). We do not send marketing email without explicit opt-in.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Threat Intelligence Providers: Submitted URLs may be sent to VirusTotal, PhishTank, URLhaus, or Spamhaus as part of analysis. This is core to the service and done without personally identifiable information.
  • Infrastructure Providers: We use cloud hosting services. Contracts with providers include data processing agreements.
  • Legal Obligations: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

Data Retention

Scan data associated with registered accounts is retained for 12 months and then automatically deleted. Anonymous scans are retained for 30 days. Account information is retained as long as the account is active. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us.

Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including TLS encryption in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest, and access controls limiting who can view raw scan data. We are SOC-2 aligned in our operational practices. No system is 100% secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Data portability (export in machine-readable format).

To exercise any of these rights, contact privacy@phishguard.io.

Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies for session management. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. See our Cookie Policy for full details.

Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@phishguard.io.
